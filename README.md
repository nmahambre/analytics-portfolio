# Nisha Mahambre | Analytics Portfolio

I’m an MS in Business Analytics student at the Isenberg School of Management at UMass Amherst, with a background in accounting and finance. These projects show how I use Python to compare financial risk, evaluate the trade-offs in fraud detection, and interpret survey data.

| Project | Question | Finding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [NVIDIA vs. Semiconductor Peers](./nvidia-peer-analysis/) | How did NVIDIA’s returns compare with its peers after accounting for risk? | From 2021–2025, NVIDIA and Broadcom had similar Sharpe ratios (1.22 and 1.16), but NVIDIA’s largest peak-to-trough decline was 66%, compared with 41% for Broadcom. |
| [Vehicle Insurance Fraud Detection](./Vehicle%20Insurance%20Fraud/) | How many fraudulent claims could a model detect, and how many legitimate claims would it flag for review? | In this team project, tuned XGBoost detected 170 of 178 fraudulent claims in the test set (95.5% recall). It also flagged 1,181 legitimate claims. |
| [Cosmetic Surgery Interest](./Cosmetic_Surgery_Regression/) | How was body satisfaction associated with interest in cosmetic surgery? | Among 170 survey respondents, a one-point increase in body satisfaction was associated with a 0.69-point lower desire score, holding the other predictors in the baseline model constant. |
